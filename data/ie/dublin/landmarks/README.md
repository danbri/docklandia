## Rules

- **Mapped minaret**: an OSM element tagged `man_made=minaret`, `building=minaret`, `building:part=minaret` or `tower:type=minaret` (way or node), or the part-stack rule: in a mosque outline, building parts of 40 m2 or less whose top is 18 m or more and 1.6 times the hall's top or more, stacked (centroids within 2.5 m). Height from OSM `height` (else the size class); the balcony (şerefe) from a part of the stack raised on `min_height` below the top part (else at 0.72 of the height); the cone from `roof:height` (else 0.2 of the height). Attached to the nearest mosque within 30 m.
- **Estimated minaret** (class "estimated", countable and switchable): estimated minarets: a mosque with no mapped minaret within 30 m gets one by the size class of its footprint; position: the outline corner nearest a mapped entrance, else the hull corner farthest along qibla + 135 deg, moved inside by the base radius. Size classes: S footprint < 150 m2: 15 m high, base 2.5 m; M footprint < 500 m2: 25 m high, base 3 m; L footprint < 1000000000 m2: 35 m high, base 3.5 m. Class S is drawn as a wooden minaret (brown cone).
- **Dome**: an OSM building part (or a worship outline without parts) with `roof:shape=dome` or `onion`: a drum from `min_height` (else the ground) to the springing, then a hemisphere (or an onion) of `roof:height` (else its radius) to `height`; radius = the smaller half side of the part's oriented box. A whole building with a dome roof outside the core: the dome stands on the city block's flat top.
- **Tower**: a building part of a worship building tagged as a tower (`building:part=tower|bell_tower|church_tower|steeple`, `tower:type=bell_tower`), or with a pointed or onion roof standing 1.25 times the body or more (12 m or more); a `man_made=tower` with `tower:type=bell_tower|clock`, `building=clock_tower|bell_tower` or named Sahat-kula. Its outline (its hull above 12 points) from `min_height` to the eaves, then a pyramid to `height` (pyramidal, cone, spire), a dome or an onion, or flat.
- **Tall thin object** (`place.landmarks.tall_objects`): an OSM element with `man_made` in chimney, obelisk, tower, monument, mast or `historic` in monument, memorial, an OSM `height` of 25 m or more, `tower:type` not communication, cooling, lighting, centroid in the box. Found by one SPARQL query to the QLever OSM endpoint (positions), then read from the OSM API boxes (tags and geometry). A `man_made=tower` with an outline of 12 m2 or more is drawn as a tower; the others as bodies of revolution: chimney (a cylinder tapering to 0.72 of its base radius), obelisk (four sides on a plinth, a pyramid point), monument (a needle to a thin top: the Spire), mast (a pole tapering to a quarter). Radius: OSM `diameter` / 2, else the outline (an obelisk's outline is its plinth: / 1.5), else a stated rule by kind (chimney 0.045, obelisk 0.06, monument 0.012, mast 0.03 x the height, clamped).
- **Ground**: a core feature's `ground_m` (+ the EGM96 geoid) where the object is a core building or part (the core walls' reference), else the drawn terrain of the finest place tile at the point. Core parts drawn here are hidden in the core tiles by the reader (no double drawing); the mosque bodies stay the place's own buildings and blocks.
- **Rendering** (architecture rule 6: geometry only, lit live): vertex colour, flat normals, the globe's sun; `_RADIAL` per vertex (the horizontal offset from the object's axis, cm) lets the reader keep a minaret at least about 1.5 CSS pixels wide at any distance (the shaft widened about its axis in the vertex shader; the height stays).
